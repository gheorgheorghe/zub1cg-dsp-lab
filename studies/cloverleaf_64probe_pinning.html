The Physics Why Fabrication Asymmetry Pins a 2D Cloverleaf

Principal Stiffness Angle:   tan(4θω) = fquad / Δf
Whole-Angle Adler Pinning Law:   dθ/dt = −AgΩz + ½Δ(1/τ) · sin(4(θ − θτ))
Dead-Band Lock Threshold:   Ωlock = Δ(1/τ) / (2 Ag)

1. Damping Pinning (θτ): Survival of the Highest-Q Mode

When one principal axis has slightly lower loss (Q1 > Q2) due to anchor asymmetry, sidewall roughness, or uneven Rtune loading, the lossier orthogonal eigenmode decays faster during ring-up. Isotropic energy sustain amplifies the surviving high-Q eigenmode — physically rotating the entire cloverleaf until its 4 antinodes lock onto θτ!

2. Why 64 Probes on the Die Are Pure Gold in HITL

With 64 radial ADCs spaced every 5.625°, a single spatial FFT directly resolves the n=2 amplitude, exact pattern angle θ(t), quadrature ellipticity Q(t), AND parasitic n=1 (lateral wobble) and n=3 modes simultaneously — letting you map θpin in seconds and dial 100 V ± 20 V trim voltages until pinning disappears!
