1 & 2 Electrostatic Softening & Duffing Nonlinearity

In capacitive parallel-plate MEMS DRGs, the electrostatic attraction force grows as 1/(d0 − x)². Expanding in a Taylor series creates both a negative linear spring (DC mode-matching) and a cubic softening term (Duffing shark-fin):

3 Whole-Angle (WA) HRG Mode — Lynch E/Q Control

In Force-to-Rebalance (FTR) mode, a high-rate maneuver (>250 °/s) can saturate the sense force loop and lose angle. In Whole-Angle (WA) mode, the standing-wave pattern is allowed to precess freely around the shell at Bryan's factor dθ/dt = −AgΩz while two strictly orthogonal Lynch loops hold total energy E constant and null quadrature Q to zero:

E = x² + vx² + y² + vy² → E0   |   Q = 2(x vy − y vx) → 0   |   Θbody = −θwave / Ag
