ZUBoard 1CG Hardware Bring-Up · Bug #02

Microchip KSZ9131RNX PHY & The 1-Bit RGMII TXC 2 ns DLL Trap

How JTAG register forensics caught two consecutive bare-metal Ethernet bugs on GEM2: an unsupported PHY OUI (0x0022) in AMD lwip220, and an asymmetric power-on strap where RX worked at 1000 Mbps while 100% of TX frames vanished on the wire.

Act 1 Unrecognized KSZ9131 PHY Crashed lwip220

Right after fixing the A53/R5F DDR4 collision (Bug #01), JTAG showed Cortex-A53 #0 halted inside Xil_Assert (PC = 0x1499c, line 0x35a) with backtrace:

main() -> netif_add() -> xemacpsif_init() -> init_emacps()
  -> get_IEEE_phy_speed() -> get_Marvell_phy_speed()  [WRONG PHY!]
  -> XEmacPs_SetOperatingSpeed(speed = 0) -> Xil_Assert()  [DEAD]

AMD's stock lwip220 (xemacpsif_physpeed.c) checks for Marvell, TI, Realtek, and ADI PHYs — but not the Avnet ZUBoard 1CG's Microchip KSZ9131RNX (PHY_ID1 = 0x0022, PHY_ID2 = 0x1642 @ MDIO addr 7). It fell through into get_Marvell_phy_speed(), read a bogus Marvell register, returned speed 0, and asserted.

Act 2 The Half-Duplex Mystery: RX = 100% Clean, TX = 0%

After patching get_Micrel_phy_speed() with standard IEEE 802.3 Clause 22 auto-negotiation, A53 booted cleanly, linked at 1000 Mbps Full Duplex, and entered its while(1) loop. Yet ping 192.168.1.10 still failed ARP!

ZUBoard GEM2 RX_FRAMES (0xFF0D0158)
19 frames · 0 FCS err
Every ARP request arrived cleanly and fired GIC IRQ
ZUBoard GEM2 TX_FRAMES (0xFF0D0108)
9 ARP replies sent
Host NIC (enx0050b6272d04) RX counter increased by 0!

That single pair of counters proved RX (PHY → Zynq GEM2) was 100% solid while TX (Zynq GEM2 → PHY) was corrupting every frame on the RGMII pins between the MPSoC and KSZ9131.

Act 3 Smoking Gun in KSZ9131 MMD 2 Registers

Reading the KSZ9131's Clause 45 MMD Device 2 registers live over JTAG through GEM2's PHY_MAINT register (0xFF0D0034) exposed the exact single-bit fault immediately:

RegisterPower-On ValueBit 12 (DLL Bypass)Result on 1000BASE-T RGMII
MMD2 0x4C (RXC DLL)0x06D10 (2 ns DLL ON)RX clock centered in eye (0 FCS err)
MMD2 0x4D (TXC DLL)0x16D11 (BYPASSED!)0 ns skew → every TX frame corrupted
At 1000BASE-T RGMII (125 MHz DDR), each nibble lasts 4.0 ns. Enabling the KSZ9131's internal 2.0 ns TXC DLL shifts sampling straight into the center of the data eye.

Permanent Fix Automated in sw/build_vitis.py

Clearing bit 12 (0x1000) on both MMD2 0x4C and MMD2 0x4D inside get_Micrel_phy_speed() (patched automatically into lwip220 on every make sw) permanently enables full rgmii-id internal delays on boot:

/* Enable RGMII-ID 2 ns DLL delays on both RXC (MMD2 0x4C) and TXC (MMD2 0x4D) */
rxc_dll = ksz9131_mmd_read(xemacpsp, phy_addr, 2, 0x4C);
txc_dll = ksz9131_mmd_read(xemacpsp, phy_addr, 2, 0x4D);
ksz9131_mmd_write(xemacpsp, phy_addr, 2, 0x4C, (u16_t)(rxc_dll & ~0x1000U));
ksz9131_mmd_write(xemacpsp, phy_addr, 2, 0x4D, (u16_t)(txc_dll & ~0x1000U));
