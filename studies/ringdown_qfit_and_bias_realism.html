100 V ± 20 V Physics Electrostatic Mode-Matching & Its Two Hidden Traps

Biasing the resonator proof mass at Vbias = 100 V and driving tuning electrodes with Vtune ∈ [−20 V, +20 V] is a classic high-authority DRG/HRG technique because the cross-term −2 Vbias Vtune gives bipolar, nearly linear frequency & quadrature control:

kelec(Vt) = −(ε0A / d0³) · (Vbias − Vtune)²   →   (100 ∓ 20)² = 6,400 to 14,400 V² (2.25× span!)
Vtune > 0 reduces softening (pushes f UP)  |  Vtune < 0 increases softening (pulls f DOWN)

Trap 1 at 100 V: Dielectric Charging & HV Reference Drift

At 100 V across micron gaps/anchors, trapped oxide charges slowly drift Veff(t) = Vbias − Vtrap(t) over minutes to days, causing turn-on bias transients and long-τ RRW. Meanwhile, ∂ke/∂V ∝ 2(Vbias−Vt) ≈ 200 V means even 1 mV (10 ppm) of HV supply ripple shifts mode split & quadrature! Fix: ultra-low-noise HV reference + periodic bipolar ±Vbias reversal.

Trap 2 at 100 V: Tuning-Resistor Ohmic Q-Loading

Adding a 100 kΩ–1 MΩ HV protection resistor on a tuning pin without an AC bypass capacitor to ground dissipates motional current as I²R heat: Qelec−1 = Rserη² / (mω0). Worse, if Vtx ≠ Vty, one axis gets damped harder than the other — injecting artificial anisodamping bias Δ(1/τ)! Fix: low-ESR shunt cap right at the package pin.

Next Realism Upgrades Major Real-World Bias Processes Not Yet in Our Modal Sim

Our current ZUBoard 1CG HIL covers Brownian ARW, ADC quantization, quadrature stiffness, anisodamping, TCF thermal drift, and slow RRW. Here are the 6 biggest remaining real-world bias mechanisms to add when hardening the model toward real flight hardware:

Bias / Realism Mechanism Physical Cause ADEV / Bias Signature How to Model in Python / HIL
1. Linear g & Quadratic g² Sensitivity (VRE) Acceleration shifts gaps (d0 ± g/ωn=1²) & modulates ke ∝ d−3; high-freq vib rectifies via 1/(d0−x)². Orientation-dependent static bias (0.05–5 °/hr/g) + sudden bias jump under random vibration! Wrap d± = d0(1±δ) ∓ (x + xg(t)) around modal plant + inject g(t) PSD.
2. Quadrature Setpoint Error + Feedthrough-Fooled CLQ Non-zero SYref or feedthrough Sft forces CLQ to hold real mechanical quad SYmech = −Sft ≠ 0, which leaks via Δφ(T) into rate! Mid-τ Bias Instability floor + strong bias vs. temperature slope. Add ymeas = y + kft·ux before demodulator + demod phase drift Δφ(T).
3. 100 V Dielectric Charging & HV Noise Stretched-exponential charge trapping in isolation oxides shifts local Veff(t) & Δf(t), kxy(t). Turn-on warmup drift (exponential over 10–1800 s) + long-τ RRW. Add multi-time-constant Vtrap(t) = Σ ci(1 − e−t/τi) into Δf, kxy.
4. Thermal Stress Hysteresis & Die-Attach Creep AuSn solder / epoxy package stress relaxes irreversibly across thermal cycles (−40 ↔ +85 °C). Bias fails to return to same value after temp cycle (uncompensated by f0(T) alone!). Add Preisach / Maxwell viscoelastic stress state driving kxy & gxy.
5. AGC Amplitude → Bias Coupling (Duffing + Quad) TIA gain or DXref drift changes xdrive, pulling f0 via Duffing β3x² & shifting nonlinear bias/SF. Scale-factor drift + corrupts PLLAdj self-thermometry calibration! Include β3x³ + slow 1/f gain drift on DX pickoff channel.
6. Magnetic (Lorentz) & Acoustic Cross-Axis Coupling F = I × B on conductive rings/bondwires in Earth/motor B-fields; neighboring gyro tones near f0 or 2f0. Heading-dependent bias (°/hr/Gauss) or coherent beat spurs in ADEV. Add umag ∝ B(t)·idrive + narrowband tone near f0.
